# Survey

Ask the people in the meeting a question, or a short set of questions, and watch the answers arrive.

## Start

The person who opens Survey in the meeting sets it up. Everyone else sees who is setting it up, then the survey as soon as it starts.

**New survey** opens the form.

- Type the question.
- Add at least two choices. **Add choice** makes another line. The × removes one.
- **One choice** means a person picks a single option. **Any number** means they can pick more than one.
- **Written answer** adds a box under the choices.
- **Add question** starts another question in the same survey.
- **Anonymous votes** shows counts and bars, and leaves names off the results.
- **Start survey** shares it with the room.

## Vote

The question and its choices come first, the results under them. Tap a choice. For a written answer, type in the box. **Save my answers** sends your ballot. You can change it and save again. The bars update for everyone.

## Close

**Close survey** stops new votes and leaves the results up. **Reopen** accepts votes again. **New survey** replaces this one. Earlier ballots stay out of the new counts.

In a hosted meeting the host leads the survey, so only their page can start, close, or replace it. Anyone in the room can still vote. In an open-floor meeting anyone can do those things, until the host taps Leading.
