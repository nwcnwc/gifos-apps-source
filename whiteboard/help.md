# Whiteboard

A shared board for workshops and meetings. Draw, put up sticky notes, vote with dots and turn the result into slides. Everyone in the room works on the same board at the same time.

## Drawing

Pick the pen and draw freehand. The highlighter draws wide, see-through strokes, good for marking up what is already there. Shapes (box, circle, diamond), lines and text are in the toolbar too. An arrow that starts or ends on a shape stays attached when you move the shape.

## Sticky notes

Use the note button on the right edge, pick a colour, then tap the board. Double-tap a note to type in it.

## Clusters

To group notes into a cluster, select several of them and press Group in the selection panel, or press Ctrl+G. A group moves as one.

## Dot voting

Select a note and press the dot button on the right edge. Press the take-back button to remove your own dot again. Everyone sees the dots as they are added.

## Frames and templates

Frames split the board into slides. Use the frame tool, or open Frames and templates and choose Empty frame. Each frame becomes one slide when you export. The same menu has a Brainstorm and a Retrospective template.

## Timer

The timer button opens a short list: 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes. Pick one and everyone sees the countdown on their own bar. Stop it from the same list.

## Working together

Open the Whiteboard in a meeting, or press Invite. Everyone who joins can edit, and people who come late get the whole board. The dot at the top of the right-edge bar shows whether your changes are shared yet. If the person who shared the board is away, your changes wait and are sent when they are back.

## Export

Export makes a PowerPoint file with one slide per frame. Notes and shapes stay editable there, and drawings come as pictures. You can also save a PNG of a frame, or a PDF with one page per frame.

## On a phone

One finger draws. Two fingers move and zoom the board.

## Privacy

The board travels only between the people in the room, end to end. No server sees it.
