# Tip GifOS Creators

GifOS is free. It sells nothing about you, and asks for no account. If it has been useful and you would like to say thanks, this app is
the tip jar.

## How to tip

Pick one of the amounts, or type your own — tips start at three dollars.
Press the big button.

Your computer then shows you the payment screen. That screen is GifOS's own,
not this app's: it names exactly who receives the money (a verified identity,
not an account number), shows the amount, and offers a way to pay — PayPal,
or USDC if you have a wallet connected. You can still change the amount
there, and you can still say no. Nothing is charged until you approve it on
that screen.

## What happens to the money

It goes to the people who build GifOS. A PayPal tip is the whole amount;
nothing is held back from it.

## What this app knows about you

Nothing. It keeps no history, no total, no name. When a tip goes through it
says thank you, and when you close it, it has forgotten you were here. If you
tip twice, it does not know about the first time.

A tip is a one-off. There is no subscription here, and nothing signs you up
for anything.

## Why every payment looks like this

Every app on GifOS that takes money uses the same payment screen you see
here. Apps never handle the payment themselves — they ask, your computer
shows you who gets what, and you decide. An app that is unsigned, or that
has been altered since its author signed it, cannot take money at all. If
you want to see what paying inside GifOS feels like before buying something
in another app, a small tip here is the tour.

## If something goes wrong

Saying no before a payment starts charges nothing — the app just offers to
try again. If the PayPal window closed, or the wait was hidden, this app
cannot tell whether PayPal charged you. If PayPal shows a charge, it was
paid. Check your PayPal activity before trying again. Payments are final:
a tip cannot be refunded from inside GifOS, which is one more reason the
approval screen always shows you the amount before anything happens.
Questions about a payment: support@gifos.app.
