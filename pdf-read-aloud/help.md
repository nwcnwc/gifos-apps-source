# Read Aloud

Read a PDF, pasted text, or a picture of text. The app reads one sentence at a time. The last thing you opened comes back the next time, on the sentence you had reached, if it fits (about 8 MB). Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Open a PDF

Press **Open**, or drag a `.pdf` onto the page. A two-page public-domain note is already here the first time, so you can press Play before you pick a file of your own.

If the file is locked, a password box appears. The password is used only to open the file on this device.

A PDF that is only a photograph of a page has no text layer. Open that picture itself, or paste it, and the app reads the words from the image.

## Paste text

Press **Paste text**, type or paste into the box, and press **Read**. You can also press Ctrl+V or Command+V anywhere outside that box. The sentences appear on the page, and the one you are on is marked.

## A picture of text

Press **Open** and choose an image, drag an image onto the page, or paste an image from the clipboard. The app shows **Reading text from image…** while it reads the words on this device. The picture stays on screen, and the sentences appear under it.

The first picture downloads English reading data (about 15 MB). After that it stays on this device. If a picture has no words, the app says so.

It reads English. A faint, skewed, or handwritten picture will come back imperfect. Check the words that matter.

## Listen

Press **Play**. The voice reads the current sentence, then the next. The sentence is marked, and a PDF page turns when the reading moves on.

**Pause** holds the sentence. **Play** continues it. **Prev** and **Next** move one sentence. On a PDF, the page box jumps to the first sentence on that page.

**Speed** is passed to the voice. It applies as the next sentence is prepared.

## The voice

The voice is not inside this app. It is whatever this computer uses for Text → speech.

Install **Offline TTS** from the store if you do not have a voice yet. Leave it in your Providers folder, and assign it to Text → speech in Settings. Then come back and press Play.

If no voice is installed, or the voice fails, a message says to install Offline TTS. The app does not play a substitute sound.

## Phone

The same controls sit at the top and wrap onto a second row. The marked sentence stays in view.

## What is saved

The last PDF or picture (when it fits), pasted text, the sentence, and the speed. Close the app, come back, they are still there.

This copy uses Mozilla's pdf.js (Apache-2.0) and Tesseract (Apache-2.0). It is unofficial — those projects did not bless it.
